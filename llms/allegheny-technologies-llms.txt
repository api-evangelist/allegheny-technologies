# ATI Inc. (Allegheny Technologies Incorporated)

> ATI Inc. is an American producer of specialty materials and components, headquartered in Dallas,
> Texas and listed on the NYSE as ATI. It melts, forges, rolls and machines titanium and titanium
> alloys, nickel-based alloys and superalloys, stainless and specialty steels, zirconium, hafnium,
> niobium and tungsten materials, plus forgings, castings and additively manufactured parts, for
> aerospace and defense, energy, chemical and hydrocarbon processing, medical and electronics
> markets. **ATI publishes no public API.** As of 2026-09-01 there is no developer portal, no
> documentation for a programmable interface, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/WSDL contract, no SDK on
> any public package registry, and no GitHub organization. This file is generated by API Evangelist
> from a third-party profile; it is not published by ATI.

## What an agent can and cannot do here

- There is nothing callable. Every ATI surface below is a human web page or a credentialed portal.
- `https://api.atimaterials.com` resolves and answers, but it serves the stock Apache "It works!"
  page and returns Tomcat 404s on every other path. It is not a documented API and must not be
  treated as one.
- `https://myati.atimaterials.com` is a customer and supplier extranet that redirects to a logon
  form. Access is granted to existing ATI customers and suppliers, not to developers.
- Customer onboarding is manual: ATI publishes PDF forms to complete and email to a sales contact.
